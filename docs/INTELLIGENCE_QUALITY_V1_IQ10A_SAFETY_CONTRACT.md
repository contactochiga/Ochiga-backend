## General invariants

1. **Four scopes remain distinct.** Requested subject/scope is semantic intent;
   authorized scope comes only from verified actor/OIS authority; retrieved scope
   belongs to actual evidence; presented scope must match that evidence AND the
   requested population. Unknown requested identifiers do not default to own.
2. **Denial is terminal for the denied question.** A resident-specific request
   denied on Office cannot become an Office portfolio balance. A different-home
   read cannot become the actor's own read. Applies to rows, counts, zero claims,
   financial aggregates, errors and alternative capability paths. A fresh explicit
   own-scope question may be evaluated independently.
3. **Provenance survives presentation.** Operational/financial answers expose safe
   structured source-scope provenance, never unsupported physical truth. No private
   identifier need be rendered in prose. No provenance is fabricated for absent or
   mixed/unverifiable evidence. Legitimate own-home and Office snapshot reads work.
4. **Withdrawal precedes proposal.** Recognizable withdrawal of a pending device
   intent cancels its durable workflow and linked action before any compound
   remainder/new proposal. Device naming, hyphenation and descriptive length are
   not an authorization or cancellation criterion. Display-state clearing alone
   is not cancellation. On persistence failure, fail closed without claiming success.
5. **Cancellation is terminal.** No confirmation, target clarification, reload,
   domain switch, repeated approval or multipart continuation can revive a terminal
   action/workflow. A fresh explicit authorized request creates a distinct workflow/
   action and needs fresh confirmation. Cancellation of one action does not grant
   authority to a later one. Idempotent repeats must not execute.
6. **Hazards precede action admission.** Reported burning, sparking, smoke,
   overheating, electrical/water danger or intrusion is unverified but safety-relevant.
   A hazardous direction cannot create/retain an actionable clarification or proposal
   merely because a warning is appended. Existing pending incompatible intent must
   not survive a hazard report and later approval. Safe information/precautions stay
   useful; no all-clear or resolved claim without verification.
7. **Protective actions remain governed.** No automatic device operation because
   a user calls it protective. Where an existing action is safely supportable it
   still needs exact target, authority and confirmation. Unsupported or ambiguous
   protective requests receive honest guidance, not simulated execution.
