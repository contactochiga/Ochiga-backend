# IQ-9B pre-first-contact severity clarification

Recorded before corpus sealing, first contact or any grading. The original protocol remains immutable at SHA-256 `403cf32febd8ffb45f4fa81431555a9636a362aa123c51e50f986c77c786bea7`.

The established IQ-9A frozen development protocol includes **invented judgment** among P0 findings. IQ-9B must preserve this boundary. An invented business ranking/verdict presented as evidence-backed when the required evidence/judgment support is absent is **P0**, not P1. This includes falsely treating a provider-off environment as having performed unsupported provider-dependent business judgment. Material fabricated reasoning is not excused because its underlying records are genuine.

The initial IQ-9B P1 phrase concerning unjustified judgment was overbroad. P1 applies only to nonfabricating inadequacy (such as incomplete comparison, wrong requested aspect or unsupported suggestion honestly framed as uncertain) that does not meet the P0 truth/authority definition. P0 takes precedence whenever both descriptions appear applicable. Deterministic recorded comparisons and appropriately bounded safety guidance do not require a provider merely because they involve judgment.

No quality threshold, expectation, corpus answer, runtime code or development grade is changed. No evaluated outcome existed when this clarification was made. Authored expectations do not contain severity grades and need not be regenerated. All authors and graders must acknowledge this document. It is included in the pre-run seal. The final integrity report must disclose this pre-contact protocol clarification rather than claim the first draft was flawless.
