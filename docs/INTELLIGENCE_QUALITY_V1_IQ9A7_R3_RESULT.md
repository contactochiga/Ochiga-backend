## Design (no new orchestrator, planner or parser)
- **Clause contract.** `AnswerTarget.clauses?: Array<{text, target}>` (answerTarget.ts). `splitInformationalClauses` (bounded to three, original order) cuts a turn at "and/, " before an interrogative, "tell me / show / list / whether" clause; a clause cut from a question keeps its question mark; an elliptical later clause ("... and which are active?") shares the first clause's object and subject. Each clause target comes from the same `deriveOneTarget`. Single questions, conditionals, "statement then question", refusals, action/communication results, confirmations, clarifications, capability questions and safety reports carry no clause list.
- **Same domain, one read.** The capability reads once; `CapabilityResponseAdapter.shapeReadAnswer` projects the single ResultEnvelope once per clause (canonical projector), dedupes identical lines, and states "I couldn't answer “…” from these records" for a clause it cannot project. Osa held-fact questions ("what have you got from me, and what do you still need?") answer every clause in order.
- **Different domains, separate governed turns.** `planMultipart` (orchestrator) parses each clause with the existing parser, groups by head domain (an elliptical clause inherits; a clause naming an unresolved subject becomes its own group and is answered or truthfully declined, never folded in; a utility purchase beside a wallet history is wallet). Each group runs as its own `runTurn`: own capability, own authority and scope decision, own result (a denied clause is stated verbatim, scope is never widened by a sibling). Bounds: three clauses, one action clause, a 25 s budget, never reads beyond what each turn does.
- **Action separation.** A read combined with a mutation answers the read first; the action clause goes through the existing governed workflow last, so it is only a proposal awaiting approval or a clarification, with "Nothing has been executed" added when no proposal exists. Two actions, or a bare yes/confirm/cancel, are not a multi-part request. Judgment shapes (prioritize, compare, advise) and whole-turn specialised shapes are never split.
- **Composition.** First answer as is, later answers "On “<clause>”: …" (action "Separately — …"); no menu, no merged summary.
- **Supporting fixes found while testing.** Office "status of VI Development" no longer parses as a device status and resolves to the development records (Office surface only; a public "project on my plot" keeps its own routing); attribution questions with "fault/offender/culprit" are refusals; Osa "how do I begin talking to the team" has a specific answer.
