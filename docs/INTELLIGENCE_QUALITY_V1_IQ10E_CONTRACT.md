## Invariants

1. Retrieval/status does not become strategic judgment. Recorded ordering is
   described as recorded ordering, not independent commercial importance.
2. A business comparison without qualified reasoning remains bounded. Present
   available named facts, missing comparable facts and the specific limitation.
3. Explanation binds to a unique named/selected or valid derived referent. Never
   choose the first record merely because it exists. Ambiguity asks clarification.
4. Unknown lifecycle is Unknown, not No. An incomplete count is a lower bound
   or returned count, not an exact population; an incomplete empty result is
   not proof of absence. State requested filters explicitly.
5. Explicit NEXT_STEP may describe verification of stale/unobserved/partial
   evidence or existing public qualification requirements. It never dispatches,
   confirms, promises contact, initiates work or invents workflow requirements.
6. Denied evidence remains denied. Unavailable evidence is not zero. Advice
   neither gains authority nor requests credentials or unnecessary personal data.
7. Lead with the requested answer. Focused explanations/limitations do not dump
   unrelated records. Preserve all material safety, scope and source limitations.
8. Derived artifacts represent only actually presented results. No invisible new
   ranking may capture subsequent ordinals. Existing IQ-5/6 scope/TTL applies.
9. Caller facts remain entity-bound and unverified; no historical reassessment
   or canonical record is overwritten. IQ-10A/B/C/D remain unchanged guarantees.
