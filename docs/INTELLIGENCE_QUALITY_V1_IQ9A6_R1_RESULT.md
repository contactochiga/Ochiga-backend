## Baseline
`artifacts/intelligence-quality-v1-iq9a6-ownership-baseline.json`: of 160 development items, 35 were answered by `oyi.assessment.evidence_plan`; 22 of them failed grading. 26 of the 35 were answerable without an assessment (retrieval, status, factual confirmation, communication truth, refusal, limitation, public held facts), 9 are genuine prioritization, ranking, comparison, advice or counterfactual questions. Honest note on process: the diagnosis artifact was written after exploratory code reading and prototype edits, not committed first; it is derived from the committed pre-change IQ-9A5 run, which predates every R1 edit.
Why the planner took over: `nextConversationAssessment` returns an assessment for any parsed assess/advise/reassess/compare/prioritize objective, and the orchestrator ran the planner unless one of a few narrow exemptions applied. The planner has no evidence class for wallet, resident, gate or Osa-without-objective subjects, so it emitted boilerplate ("could not be read", "nothing to compare").

## Planner admission contract
`orchestration/plannerAdmission.ts` (pure): `plannerAdmission({surface, target, objective, continuing, mutationOrAction, subjectDomains, eligibleReadDomains, headDomainSupportedOnSurface, headDomain, conceptDomains, hasPublicObjective, specificCapability}) -> {admit, reason}`. Order: (1) a genuine continuation of an unsuspended assessment is admitted; (2) a governed action request, or a specialised answer shape (ACTION_RESULT, CONFIRMATION_STATE, CLARIFICATION, REFUSAL, CAPABILITY_DISCOVERY, LIMITATION) is declined; (3) Osa without an established opportunity objective is declined (the planner's only Osa class is the held objective), and the orchestrator then answers with a specific public limitation, or hands a turn that states opportunity facts to the opportunity module, or keeps the planner's evidence statement; (4) a retrieval shape (list, count, status, direct answer, state/fact/change yes-no) is declined when an allowed, non-global read owns the question's single head domain and the objective is not prioritize, compare or advise; (5) a head domain that belongs to another surface, has no read on this surface and no specific owner is declined; (6) wallet and transaction subjects, for which the planner has no class at all, are declined; otherwise admitted. Admission and reason are recorded as the structural trace stage `planner_admission` (closed reason set, projected as `planner_admitted` and `planner_admission_reason`), plus an `oyi_planner_admission` log line with no prompt or evidence text. No phrase-specific routing was added. Supporting target changes: "did <owner> get my message? check delivery" is an ACTION_RESULT (communication); the Osa module answers a statement-plus-question turn from the facts it just recorded.
