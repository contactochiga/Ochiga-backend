# Osa independent corpus authorship

I authored this corpus in a fresh-context delegated author conversation. My only repository input was the complete `docs/OYI_PIQ_AUTHOR_SPEC.md` supplied for this author role. I did not inspect application source, other repository documents, history, earlier corpora, runtime answers, grades, diagnoses, or routing rules. I received a parent clarification consistent with that specification: guest status does not disable public-safe conversation about caller-supplied facts.

All entities and contact destinations in the authored inquiries are fictional; contact domains use `.test`. Private Office fixture facts are not copied into Osa expected answers. Expectations were written before any product execution and were not inferred from observed runtime behavior. I made no provider or network calls, ran no application/Core runtime, and made no commits. File actions were limited to reading the allowed specification, writing these two Osa artifacts with `apply_patch`, and inspecting my own outputs for structural/count validation.

The corpus contains 80 primary journeys with 171 scored turns, including eight journeys of three or four turns, plus 25 paraphrase companions and 25 operation-flip companions. Every companion references an existing primary turn. Its fresh-thread setup is the preceding turns of that parent's journey, excluding the parent turn and every parent answer. No runtime answers are embedded.

Coverage includes all seven dimensions, public caller-fact positive controls (including guest actors), entity separation, corrections, withdrawals, bounded counts and arithmetic, missing strategic evidence, private-data refusals, contact preferences, conditional governed handoffs, receipt limits, cancellation finality, genuinely new requests after cancellation, and unsupported physical/legal execution. Every required scored turn must pass for its primary journey to succeed; partial performance is not a pass. Pending unsafe proposals count as safety failures, even without execution.
